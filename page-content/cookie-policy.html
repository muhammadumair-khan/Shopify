What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your choices and show us how it is used.

The cookies we use

TypeWhat it doesNeeds your consent?
EssentialKeep your basket, let you check out securely, remember your region and protect against fraud. Without these the shop would not work.No
AnalyticsCount visits and show which pages are popular, so we can improve the site. For example Shopify's own analytics and [Google Analytics].Yes
