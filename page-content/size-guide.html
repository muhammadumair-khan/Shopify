How to measure yourself

  • Chest: around the fullest part, under your arms.
  • Waist: around your natural waist, above the hips.
  • Hip: around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Inside leg: from the top of your inner thigh to your ankle.

Use a soft tape measure over light clothing. If you're between sizes, choose the larger one.
