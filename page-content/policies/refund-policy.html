30-day returns and exchanges

Changed your mind or ordered the wrong size? You have 30 days from the day your order arrives to return or exchange it.

What we accept

  • Items that are unworn, unwashed and have their original tags.
  • Items in their original packaging where possible.

How to return or exchange

  1. Contact us through our contact page with your order number and whether you want a refund or a different size.
  2. We'll send you the return details [and a prepaid label for exchanges].
  3. Post the item back to us. Please keep your proof of postage.
  4. Once we receive it, we send your exchange or refund your original payment method within [5] working days. Refunds may take a few more days to show in your account.

Return postage

[Exchanges for a different size are free. For refunds, return postage is £[x.xx] and is deducted from your refund / paid by you.] Faulty items are always free to return.

Faulty or misdescribed items

If something is faulty or not as described, contact us as soon as you can. You are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and we will cover the cost of returning it.

These terms are in addition to, and do not affect, your statutory rights, including your right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.
