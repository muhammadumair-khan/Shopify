Replace everything in [square brackets] with your real details, and check that each statement is true for your business. This is general information, not legal advice; get your policies checked if you are unsure.

## Legal policies (`policies/` folder)
Shopify has a special place for these: **Settings → Policies**. Paste each draft into its box (click the `<>` Show HTML button first, and delete the `<!-- DRAFT ... -->` line at the top):

| File | Where it goes | Page address |
|---|---|---|
| `policies/privacy-policy.html` | Settings → Policies → Privacy policy | `/policies/privacy-policy` |
| `policies/terms-of-service.html` | Settings → Policies → Terms of service | `/policies/terms-of-service` |
| `policies/refund-policy.html` | Settings → Policies → Refund policy | `/policies/refund-policy` |
| `policies/shipping-policy.html` | Settings → Policies → Shipping policy | `/policies/shipping-policy` |
| `cookie-policy.html` | Pages → Add page, title **Cookie policy** | `/pages/cookie-policy` |

Shopify links these in checkout automatically, and the theme footer lists every policy you save. Pages the drafts link to: `/pages/contact-us` (your Contact page; if its address is different, change the links) and `/pages/delivery` (the Delivery page).

**Before you publish them:** fill in every [square bracket], remove anything that doesn't apply, and have a solicitor or your trade body check them. Also:
- Register with the **ICO** and pay the data protection fee unless you're exempt (ico.org.uk/fee-checker), then put your registration number in the privacy policy.
- Turn on the cookie banner (Settings → Customer privacy) so your cookie policy matches what visitors see.
- Keep the policies true: if you add apps or tools that collect data (reviews, email, ads), add them to the privacy and cookie policies.
